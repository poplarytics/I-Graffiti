# Soft-Cell Freight Funk

## Purpose

Create an original, materially flat aerosol graffiti word on one continuous real near-frontal carrier. A freight car is one valid carrier, not a requirement; exterior building walls and other credible continuous surfaces are allowed. The word must read first as a low connected band of piled inflated cells, not as evenly spaced bubble letters or a copy of a source writing style.

## Input handling

- Preserve a supplied word or mark as one compressed collective rhythm. With no supplied word, use `Graffiti`.
- For a word longer than four letters, preserve only a few distributed local reading anchors. For `MOTION`, use a double-peak M entry, one open O bowl, a shared T bridge, and a slanted N terminal; each must be visibly interrupted by shared mass or a corridor, never completed as an isolated letter.
- Infer carrier, weather, crop, and lighting without changing the style grammar.
- Never use a source artist name, source word, signature, tag, mark, exact palette, or exact composition.

## Construction sequence

1. Paint one original low horizontal word-band on a continuous real carrier in a near-frontal view. Make the word's rhythm legible, but let adjacent regions crop, overlap, and share mass rather than tracing each glyph's outside edge.
2. Build most mass from inflated lobes, pill compartments, broad hooks, rounded bowls, U-turns, and soft terminals. Make an upper bank and a lower bank around the centerline, overlapping vertically by at least one third of the full band height; use at least three major cells that visibly cross between banks. Let at least two adjacent letter regions be obscured by a shared crossing cell. Preserve this stack on pale or dark carriers—never restore a continuous single baseline.
3. Add at least two broad contrasting corridors that travel through the combined pile and visibly bridge upper and lower banks before terminating inside another cell. Keep both contained within the word-band outer silhouette and visibly separate; neither may become a long sweep beyond the contour. Let their width and direction respond to the word; do not let them merely trace glyph outlines. A corridor may be an outline, separator, or bridge, but it must never become a full rectangular border or a consistently shifted duplicate word layer.
4. Add dark internal turns, cutbacks, and compact open or pinched counter relations only where they explain a cell's direction. Keep them locally thick and graphic. Do not use them as a fine-line counter-replacement system or as decorative scribble.
5. Choose one compact role hierarchy from the selected reference family: dark contour, dominant fill, structural corridor, and up to two accent roles. Local highlight or underside shifts may follow a cell curve but must remain flat paint, not physical depth.
6. Add one local grouped dot cluster. Then choose at most two supporting vocabularies from a small star, short drop, or low bubble/cloud backfill. Attach them to local cell relations or behind the word mass; never pattern-fill every cell.
7. Keep carrier-specific seams, ribs, masonry, hatches, hardware, weathering, and existing markings credible where visible. Paint remains flat and near-frontal with no cast shadows, detached pieces, or global perspective system.

## Guardrails

Avoid clean display type, even bubble-letter spacing, separate complete glyph outlines, sharp mechanical facets, structural grids, long ribbon loops, woven passes, a shifted duplicate back layer, spatial misregistration, a complete rectangular painted field, regular geometric modules, fine-line gap substitution, fixed palettes, rainbow haze, dense dots or stars, characters, signage, relief, sidewalls, or volumetric depth.

This style differs from Style 001 through swollen cells instead of mechanical architecture; from Style 002 through compact local cell curves instead of long ribbon flow; from Style 003 through in-place variable corridors instead of a displaced duplicate layer; and from Style 004 through an irregular soft-cell band instead of a rectangular geometric field.

## Completion check

Confirm that one shallow connected word-band dominates, upper/lower cells visibly pile together, corridors reconnect rather than echo the word, dark marks clarify local turns, accents remain sparse, and the carrier still reads as a real flat painted surface. Reject any run dominated by a predecessor grammar.
