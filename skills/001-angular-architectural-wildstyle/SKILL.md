# Angular Architectural Wildstyle Skill

## Purpose

Create original Angular Architectural Wildstyle as materially flat aerosol paint on one continuous substrate. Borrow assembled/mechanical visual grammar, but make all depth optical through painted illusion only.

## Input handling

- With a supplied word or mark, use it as the subject and retain a coherent word-level silhouette; individual letters need not be plainly readable.
- With partial direction, infer only ordinary scene variables; preserve all style invariants.
- With no subject, use the default word `Graffiti`. Do not invent an additional slogan, crown, smiley face, or star.

## Construction order

1. Lay out an interconnected angular letter skeleton while preserving the word-level silhouette.
2. Depict mechanical joints, wedges, and faceted internal planes as flat painted shapes.
3. Paint apparent extrusion, overlaps, recesses, and layered outlines for optical depth only.
4. Assign a three-to-five-color hierarchy by visual role, including a painted depth-shadow role.
5. Apply authentic aerosol deposition: vary edge softness/hardness with irregular modulation, use non-uniform opacity, and allow only restrained localized overspray.
6. Carry cracks, pores, pits, seams, mortar, and relief continuously through every painted color region.
7. Match substrate perspective, scale, and lighting without introducing real thickness, sidewalls, detached elements, gaps, contact shadows, ambient-occlusion seams, or environmental cast shadows.

## Guardrails

Keep the result dense but intentional and materially flat. Avoid bubble typography, rainbow fills, excessive splatter, generic symbols, random text, flat digital compositing, physical slabs or panels, metal components, wall sculptures, riveted signage, CG hard-beveled panels, real sidewalls, detached pieces, separation gaps, contact shadows, ambient-occlusion seams, and environmental cast shadows.

## Completion check

Before delivery, confirm that substrate topology crosses every colour region and that all apparent depth comes only from painted shapes. Any real thickness, sidewall, detachment, gap, contact shadow, ambient-occlusion seam, or environmental cast shadow fails the style and must be removed.
