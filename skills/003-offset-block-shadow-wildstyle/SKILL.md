# Offset Block-Shadow Wildstyle

## Purpose

Create an original flat aerosol mural whose primary word remains a clear modular silhouette while a large displaced painted back layer establishes spatial depth.

## Input handling

- With a supplied word, preserve one coherent word-group mass; individual letters may be substantially abstracted and need not be plainly readable.
- With no supplied word, use `Graffiti`.
- Randomly use no decoration or one subordinate star, cross-star, or flame-state motif. Do not add a character or a fixed slogan.

## Construction sequence

1. Build thick modular foreground letters with compressed spacing, selective overlap, irregular local rhythm, inner shadows, cut-ins, stepped blocks, traceable counters, and wide arc-blocks or blocky bends; avoid clean font-like spacing and pointed crown silhouettes.
2. Add one large painted back-layer system that emerges through counters, cuts, overlaps, and edges; it must interlock with the letter skeleton and be visibly larger than a normal narrow drop shadow.
3. Use controlled reveals, overlaps, cross-grid shifts, and partial occlusion to create organized spatial misregistration.
4. Assign two or three foreground hues, a dark structural role, a pale keyline/highlight role, and one contrasting back-layer role.
5. Add only localized soft aerosol or optional subordinate decoration after the foreground/back-layer structure is clear.
6. Keep cracks, seams, pores, corrugation, and carrier perspective continuous through every painted region.

## Guardrails

Keep graffiti-native modular bodies plus interlocking offset back-layer displacement dominant. Let wide arc-blocks, blocky bends, and square-capped discontinuities outweigh triangular blade planes. Avoid clean font-like spacing, detached background grids, mechanical joints, faceted wedges, spear terminals, pointed crowns, long ribbons, loops, hooks, woven cross-letter sweeps, ordinary narrow shadows, bubble-only construction, copied source details, decoration dominance, physical signs, relief, or floating dimensional parts.

## Completion check

Reject any run with lost graffiti-native word rhythm, a detached back-layer grid, loss of the word-level silhouette, a narrow ordinary shadow, convergence into architectural or ribbon-flow construction, source copying, or physical dimensionality.
