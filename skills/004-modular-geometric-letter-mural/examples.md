# Examples

## Zero-input invocation

```text
Use $modular-geometric-letter-mural.
```

Expected behavior: create an original `Graffiti` mural as an interlocked word field of overlapping rounded blocks and long contained segments. A dark scaffold and the palette family vary by reference-led selection; thin lines replace selected gaps and no individual glyph perimeter is traced.

## Supplied word

```text
Use $modular-geometric-letter-mural for "RHYTHM" on a near-frontal weathered concrete wall. Use a black/cream/stone/olive role family: let black form selected perimeter and absorbed counters, let pale and stone blocks dominate, and use no focal transition.
```

Expected behavior: preserve `RHYTHM` as a collective silhouette while overlapping strokes, eliminating separate letter outlines, using large rounded modules, and keeping the dark scaffold selective rather than dominant.

## Palette-family variation

```text
Use $modular-geometric-letter-mural for "STATIC" with a black/cream/rust/burgundy/navy family. Use dark paint as a structural scaffold, with cream and rust/burgundy major blocks; reserve a muted burgundy-to-rust transition for one focal module.
```

Expected behavior: retain the same geometric construction and line logic while varying the reference-led palette roles; it must not reuse a fixed default scheme, and it may use many near-flat blocks.

## Counter-gap pressure case

```text
Use $modular-geometric-letter-mural for "MNH". Use a near-black burgundy base and an aubergine-to-plum-to-magenta/coral trajectory, with no blue. Compress the M, N, and H internal gaps through local block distortion; use tightly attached fine lines in place of those gaps.
```

Expected behavior: the letter family stays collectively interlocked. Each selected internal gap is created by a local transformation and is marked by its own attached line system, not by a conventional large hole or free decoration.

## Fine-line shadow treatment

```text
Use $modular-geometric-letter-mural to paint "MOTION" on an aged transit-wall panel. Use fine parallel line bands for selected internal gap and shadow indications; keep all other decoration sparse.
```

Expected behavior: lines clarify shallow graphic structure without becoming a perspective grid, a ribbon system, or a separate offset shadow layer.
