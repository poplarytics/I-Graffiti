# Balanced Ribbon-Flow Wildstyle

## Purpose

Create an original, horizontally balanced wildstyle mural whose principal word reads as one flowing painted silhouette on a real carrier.

## Input handling

- With a supplied word, preserve its coherent word-level silhouette; individual letters need not be plainly recoverable.
- With partial direction, infer ordinary scene variables while preserving every invariant.
- With no subject, use `Graffiti`. Randomly choose whether the output includes no auxiliary element or one subordinate character, icon, tag, or freshly generated short slogan. Never reuse a canonical slogan.

## Construction sequence

1. Establish a distinctly wide word-level silhouette with a dense center and long extensions toward both sides.
2. Build the primary skeleton from meaningful alternation between broad straight or angled bars and S-curves, C-curves, hooks, loops, and ribbon connectors.
3. Carry selected strokes across more than one letter region through overlaps, passes, sweeping terminals, and directional echoes.
4. Balance vertical reach, color weight, extensions, interior channels, and outer breathing room without mechanical mirror symmetry.
5. Order the painted weave with front-back occlusion, offset color bands, dark underlayers, inset shadows, and pale keylines.
6. Assign two or three related main hues, a dark structural role, a pale keyline/highlight role, and at most one contrasting peripheral accent.
7. Keep the structural edges hard; localize soft spray, gradients, glows, starbursts, dots, drips, or mist so the skeleton remains clear.
8. If decoration is selected, vary its type per output and keep the character, icon, tag, or fresh short slogan subordinate to the word group.
9. Integrate the painting with a real wall, vehicle, or other physical carrier in a near-frontal to moderately oblique view while preserving its wide silhouette.

## Guardrails

Keep ribbon flow and straight-curve alternation dominant. Avoid Style 001's architectural or mechanical organization, all-angular construction, all-rounded bubble construction, disconnected letters, narrow stacking, unstructured rainbow color, effect overload, dominant decoration, copied source text, signatures, characters, or compositions, and any fabricated signage, relief lettering, sculpture, or floating dimensional object.

## Completion check

Confirm a wide word-level silhouette, meaningful straight-curve alternation, and ordered painted overlap. Reject any run that becomes architectural, bubble-only, physically dimensional, or dominated by effects or decoration.
