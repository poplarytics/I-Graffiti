# Baseline Prompt — Draft

Create an original near-frontal documentary photograph of a graffiti word field based on “Graffiti” painted on one continuous weathered wall. The wall may be textured or physically uneven, including brick, rough concrete, or weathered stucco; keep the paint flat, matte, and physically plausible on that surface. Make oversized, only partly legible glyph masses—cropped bowls, hooks, bars, blocks, and terminals—then send broad hard-edged colored faces across neighboring glyph zones to interrupt normal stroke continuity. At selected crossings, use only short dark cutbacks or small contrasting underside planes to explain which face passes in front. Add a broad irregular painted ground behind the field, subordinate to the lettering. Retain distributed local reading anchors, but avoid typeset complete display letters, painted relief, detached pieces, or a mandatory shared outer silhouette.

Do not use a unified offset duplicate layer, global 3D extrusion, a complete rectangular panel governing the composition, a dark scaffold, mechanical joints, long flowing ribbons, fine-line gap systems, piled soft cells, source-specific words, tags, signatures, palettes, compositions, or watermarks.
