# Examples

These prompts demonstrate transfer of the same invariants without reproducing reference text or source compositions.

## Zero input

```text
Create the zero-input Balanced Ribbon-Flow Wildstyle fallback: “Graffiti” as a complete mural on a weathered charcoal-prepared concrete wall, near-frontal in neutral overcast daylight and a wide landscape frame. Use coral pink and warm red main forms, pale blush offset bands, deep plum structure, cream keylines, and turquoise only as a peripheral accent. Randomly select no auxiliary element or one subordinate character, icon, tag, or freshly generated short slogan. Keep the wide ribbon-flow word group dominant.
```

## Supplied-word carrier transfer

```text
Create “RHYTHM” as a wide Balanced Ribbon-Flow Wildstyle mural across the side of a city bus, with real panel seams passing through the paint. Keep a near-frontal wide landscape view, neutral overcast daylight, and the coral pink, warm red, pale blush, deep plum, turquoise, and cream role hierarchy. Alternate broad bars with S-curves, C-curves, hooks, loops, and ribbon connectors; carry selected strokes across multiple letter regions and preserve the coherent word-level silhouette.
```

## Controlled decoration

```text
Create an original wide “RHYTHM” mural on a weathered charcoal-prepared concrete wall using the same balanced ribbon-flow skeleton and role-based palette. Add one randomly selected subordinate character or icon plus a fresh short slogan generated specifically for this output. Keep both auxiliary elements peripheral and smaller than the word group; do not copy source text, signatures, characters, or compositions, and do not let architectural-mechanical grammar dominate.
```
