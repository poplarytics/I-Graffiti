# Examples

## Zero-input invocation

```text
Use $soft-cell-freight-funk.
```

Expected behavior: create an original `Graffiti` treatment as one shallow connected soft-cell band on a credible continuous near-frontal real carrier. It uses piled upper/lower cells, variable structural corridors, selected dark internal turns, and one local dot cluster.

## Supplied word and carrier transfer

```text
Use $soft-cell-freight-funk for "MOTION" on a weathered exterior wall of an abandoned industrial building in bright overcast daylight. Use a compact palette with an aubergine contour role, pale mint dominant fill, cream corridor role, and small coral accents. Include one local dot cluster and one small star.
```

Expected behavior: `MOTION` remains a recoverable low collective rhythm without isolated glyph outlines. The pale corridor bridges cells in place; it does not become a duplicate offset word. The star remains one local accent.

## Boundary pressure

```text
Use $soft-cell-freight-funk for "MNH" on a pale ribbed freight-car side. Keep the word low and compact, with dark internal turns and one dot cluster. This is an allowed carrier choice, not a style requirement. Do not use facets, ribbons, offset shadows, a rectangular painted field, or fine-line counter substitution.
```

Expected behavior: the result remains a pile of soft cells in two registers, with variable broad corridors and no predecessor grammar taking control.
